Our Story

Born from a passion
for the perfect cup

In the heart of Kloof, our coffee shop began as a dream nurtured in a small kitchen — a fervent love for coffee and a desire to create a warm haven from the everyday. Through sleepless nights, endless experiments with blends and brewing techniques, we built something real.

Today, Malcolm&Co. is more than a café. It's a corner of the world where the aroma of freshly ground beans signals the start of something good, where every cup carries the weight of that original dream.

Philosophy
03
01 — SOURCE
Where it begins

We establish direct relationships with growers across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Brazil — ensuring fair trade practices and sustainable farming that benefits the soil and the people who tend it.

02 — ROAST
The art of transformation

Our roasts are small-batch and carefully profiled. We aim to reveal the bean's natural character rather than impose a house style — terroir in every cup.

03 — SERVE
Ritual, not routine

Every cup prepared at Malcolm&Co. is an act of care. Our baristas are trained not just in technique but in the philosophy of hospitality — slow down, taste, connect.
